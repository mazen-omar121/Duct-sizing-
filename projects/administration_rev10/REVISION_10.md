# Administration HVAC Rev10 — routing and fitting revision

Rev10 replaces the published Rev09 routing proposal in response to the supplied connection examples. It is an engineering coordination proposal, with elevations and final selections pending.

- Connected service outlines are rendered from a single exterior after all fills. A later runout cannot erase a header wall. Adapters replace the constant-width throat; local fitting radii are formed in the actual envelope.
- No unintended same-service polygon overlap, duplicated centerline or retraced header is allowed. Every registered joint conserves airflow.
- Corridor supply distribution and return collection use real aggregate-flow headers instead of overlapping individual round runouts.
- Meeting, telecom, operator, toilet, bed-room and clean-agent paths are separated. The operator return follows the perimeter; the clean-agent supply avoids the kitchen supply branch.
- There are 122 scheduled sections, 53 terminal duties and 85 instrument requirements. Room duties, terminal neck / face sizes and I/O allocations are retained.
- Proposed section size changes and 22 terminal relocations are recorded in Rev10_route_changes.csv, Rev10_size_changes.csv and Rev10_terminal_changes.csv and on D-021. The largest proposed clear duct depth is 800 mm. These changes need ceiling, diffuser performance, fittings / ESP and engineering approval.
- D-019 contains six enlargements of actual delivered junction geometry. D-020 indexes all 20 separate-service crossings with dashed lower edges and a section height-envelope check against the confirmed 4 m structural underside. D-021 lists terminal / section revisions. The detailed package contains 21 A1 sheets plus the four functional schematic sheets.
- Actuator / heater glyphs are placed on checked straight branch stations outside crossing footprints. The selected OEM face-to-face dimensions, actuator access envelopes and compartment fire ratings remain pending.

## Remaining coordination

The user confirmed a 4.000 m structural slab/roof underside. The AFFL reference requires site verification. Finished suspended ceiling and actual duct BODs remain unassigned. External insulation is assumed at 50 mm for the indoor coordination check; this is not a verified UAE average or project specification. The proposed 100 mm gap between insulation surfaces and 100 mm structure/hanger allowance require project/OEM confirmation.

The 20 crossings have calculated maximum local BOD bounds and required stack heights in the crossing register. These are not connected-network elevation assignments. CX-08 has 750 mm supply and 800 mm return depths: the assumed stack requires 1950 mm and leaves the lowest insulated surface at +2.050 m. Resolve a shallower crossing section or reroute against the required finished ceiling and working access before construction. Final width/height conversions, fitting losses, supports, beams and terminal plenums remain open.

Pressure / outdoor-air balance, fitting coefficients, fan ESP / curves, catalogue selections, approved fire boundaries and I&C settings / wiring remain open. The Rev08 pressure / OA issues are not closed by routing changes. Original Rev08 and published Rev09 source / deliverables are retained.
