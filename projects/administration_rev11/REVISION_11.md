# Rev11: 3.30 m ceiling / roof distribution option

User datums: underside of the structural slab/roof at +4.000 m and minimum finished ceiling / clear headroom +3.300 m. This leaves 700 mm. Rev10 CX-08's lowest insulated surface at +2.050 m fails the requirement by 1250 mm; the old stacked arrangement is superseded for this option.

Rev11 proposes common supply/return distribution on the roof with 15 independent room drops. **Option selection is pending.** A physical roof route has not been designed without a roof survey. Roof main flows and clear sizes are a functional basis, separated from the indoor measured section schedule. All new slab penetrations require structural/architectural coordination, selected sleeves/curbs, weatherproofing, fire ratings, access and roof loads.

The indoor proposal contains 116 section paths: nine supply, six return and two extract connected room trees. Perimeter returns in the electrical and telecom rooms replace return cross-bars through the supply trees. No bare indoor service crossings, insulated separate-service overlaps, unregistered same-service overlaps or retraced centerlines are permitted. Every terminal connects to its allocated room tree; each tree ends at its registered riser or dedicated extract outlet. Scheduled node flows conserve airflow.

All indoor trees use a proposed bare bottom level of +3.400 m. Bare depths are at most 400 mm. Assumed indoor external insulation is 50 mm each face, **not a verified UAE standard/specification**. The deepest insulated envelope is +3.350 to +3.850 m, leaving 50 mm above the ceiling and 150 mm below the slab. This checks duct envelopes only: selected flanges/stiffeners, supports, actuators, heater casing, terminal plenums/necks and working access may require local redesign. The 50 mm ceiling gap is not a general plenum or maintenance allowance. Installed BOD remains unassigned; AFFL and beam downstands require site confirmation.

Reference glyph cores for 35 inline devices and 20 indoor access doors are checked against actual branch geometry. A proposed 200–300 mm separation is reserved between each inline device and the room's vertical-drop elbow, based on duct depth. Selected elbow development, device face-to-face lengths, actuation and maintenance access remain pending. AD-HCOM-01 remains in the roof-plant functional drawing with physical coordinates TBC.

The package retains 53 terminal duties, neck and face sizes, 85 instrument requirements and all I/O duties from Rev10. Thirteen additional terminal position proposals and twenty existing section size changes are logged against Rev10. New perimeter header sections are in the route log. All 116 indoor velocities, horizontal centerline lengths and Colebrook straight-duct friction values are recomputed. Actual roof/riser lengths, fitting K and revised system ESP/fan curves remain open.
