# Administration HVAC Rev12 — engineering development

Based on published Rev11 (`9998266`). User confirmed structural underside +4.000 m AFFL, minimum ceiling +3.300 m, and roof distribution with room drops.

The package adds ten A1 sheets D-023 through D-032: scaled roof routing, two critical sections, fitting development, terminal-path pressure estimates and fan budgets, room air balance and diffuser selection criteria, instrument mounting and wiring, roof curbs/supports, component envelopes and native roof setting-out dimensions. The actual 32-sheet index replaces D-018. Four functional schematic sheets are retained.

31 roof segments connect 15 room drops. Supply branches are placed to avoid the six upper return riser shafts; the 11 registered plan overlaps have vertically separate insulated envelopes. No same-service unconnected roof overlaps are accepted. These are proposed routes on the supplied floor footprint, not a surveyed roof. PAU reservation footprints and dashed nozzle/manifold interfaces are provisional; their detailed route losses remain open.

Roof surface +4.200 m assumes a 200 mm slab above the confirmed underside. Supply bare BOD +4.800 m, return +5.550 m, outdoor insulation 75 mm each face are proposals. The supply main is 600 x 1300 mm and return main 700 x 900 mm; tall rooftop ducts require structural wind/bracing and maintenance-platform review. Actual beam, slab, parapet and plant dimensions were not supplied. The CURB-RS-RA05 reservation at X=19500 mm extends 25 mm past the nominal 20000 mm floor-plan outline; verify the actual roof/parapet and shift the coordinated shaft or revise its opening detail before any penetration approval. Minimum registered roof crossing insulation clearance is 150 mm. Fifteen curbs reserve duct plus insulation plus 100 mm annular installation allowance; the 300 mm curb upstand and 1500 mm support spacing are proposals, not approved structural designs.

52 terminal pressure paths include proposed roof plan lengths, riser rises and unchanged indoor centerlines. Straight friction uses Colebrook/Darcy-Weisbach (rho 1.2 kg/m3, mu 1.81e-5 Pa.s, roughness 0.09 mm). All local K and component loss allowances are explicitly assumed, with 50–150% sensitivity. The PAU budget adds worst SA and RA paths plus 20% margin. No fan is selected: nozzle/manifold, OA intake, exhaust discharge, dirty-filter convention and OEM losses/curves remain pending. CAG extract has no numerical pressure path because its duty is unresolved.

The mass-balance minimum makeup is 691.12 L/s versus the 420.70 L/s OA reference (shortfall 270.42 L/s). Known extract is 212.49 L/s; residual transfer/exfiltration is 478.63 L/s less unresolved CAG extract. Toilet net -18.69 L/s and kitchen zero do not establish +25 Pa. Duties are retained while these conflicts are recorded; no fabricated leakage measurement or pressure certification is presented.

All 53 terminal selections have neck-velocity/spacing screens and OEM throw/NC/loss fields. Half-spacing is an indicative coverage distance, not certified throw. Proposed NC targets require owner/acoustic confirmation. All 85 instruments receive mounting, ranges, taps, wiring and access specifications; their requirements and I/O duties are unchanged. Ranges and interface circuits are proposals; final OEM terminals, safety settings and cable routes remain pending.

Indoor bare BOD +3.400 m, 50 mm insulation and maximum bare depth 400 mm are retained. Adding a proposed 25 mm flange allowance each side gives +3.325 to +3.875 m at the deepest duct, leaving 25 mm ceiling gap and 125 mm slab allowance. Actuators, beams, hangers and actual OEM access can still require redesign. Heater casing+insulation procurement limit is 500 mm overall; illustrative terminal plenum limit 350 mm requires a coordinated branch connection.

The 116 indoor paths, 53 terminal duties/neck/face sizes, 85 instrument requirements, unchanged I/O and Rev11 recorded moves are preserved. Rev08–Rev11 source and deliveries remain intact. The supplied benchmark DWG is unchanged. The new deliverable is editable DXF; use AutoCAD Open and Save As DWG. Native AutoCAD plotting has not been executed.

**For engineering coordination, not for construction.** Structural, fire, roof survey, manufacturer selection and independent professional approval remain required before release.
